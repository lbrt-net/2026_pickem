# Bidding guide — what a player is worth to you right now

Auction drafts, lineup **G / F / C / TM / FLX** (FLX = any player or an NBA team), budget **$200**, minimum bid **$1**. Projections: PROJ MAX for players (counted at the 86% of weeks they actually play), TEAM scoring draft 6 for NBA teams (they play every week). Clutch scoring is not in these numbers yet. Running doc, started 2026-10-07.

## The formula

For the player on the block:

1. **Which slot he'd fill on your roster**: his own position (G, F or C; an NBA team → TM) if that slot is open, otherwise FLX if FLX is open. Neither open → he's worth **$0** to you (no bench).
2. **Points over replacement** for that slot = his weekly value − the replacement level for that slot (table below). Weekly value = PROJ MAX × 0.86 for a player, the TEAM projection for a team.
3. **Fair price** = $1 + points over replacement × the **going rate** (dollars per point a week, below).
4. **Your money adjustment**: multiply by *(your dollars − $1 × your open slots) ÷ (your open slots × $39)*. $39 is the average money above the minimum per slot at the start ($195 ÷ 5). Flush with money → bid up; short → bid down.
5. **Never bid more than** your dollars − $1 × (your open slots − 1), so you can still fill every slot.

The going rate is all the league's money above the $1 minimums divided by all the starters' points over replacement, so fair prices of every starter add up to exactly the league's money (checked below).

## Reading it

- **Centers and guards are where the money goes.** Good centers are scarce (replacement center is far below the top ones), so Jokić and Wembanyama are worth the most. Forwards are plentiful — Tatum is the only forward far above replacement.
- **FLX is cheap.** Its replacement is the best player left of any position, so a player bought only for FLX is worth much less: Tatum $65 in his own slot vs $45 in FLX at 4 teams; Davis $24 vs $4.
- **Your second guard or center is a FLX buy.** Once your G slot is filled, the next guard is priced off the FLX column, not his own.
- **TEAMs**: fill TM; the average starting TEAM is worth about a forward ($28–29 vs $26–28). A TEAM in FLX is worth $0 — players beat teams for FLX.
- **Small leagues flatten everything**: at 4 teams only the top ~15 players are worth real money; at 8, about the top 40.
